# Research digest, Wednesday, November 18

- Madison & Wall's data showing creator marketing pulling budgets from TV, and brands paying creators for the ads they produce, signals that creator-made video is becoming a paid service you can sell to brands (https://videoweek.com/2026/11/18/creator-marketing-is-cannibalising-tv-spend-finds-madison-wall).
- Digiday reports that brands are booking creators to make their ads, with the US growing 15% this year and India the fastest at 20%, which points to a growing market for AI video production work (https://digiday.com/media-buying/brands-booking-creators-to-make-their-ads-are-pushing-up-116b-global-influencer-market).
- Microsoft's push for cheaper specialized models at a fraction of frontier-model cost could lower the price of the AI tools you use to generate scripts, images and video (https://www.axios.com/2026/11/17/microsoft-ai-models-openai-anthropic-ignite).
- SiMa.ai's Palette 3.0 claims up to 3.5x faster on-device language model output, a sign that AI tools will keep getting faster and cheaper to run for content workflows (https://thenextweb.com/news/sima-ai-palette-3-0-physical-ai-drones-robots).
- Reddit and Instagram-focused posts argue that new accounts should prioritize Reels, since Reels reach non-followers and need a strong hook in the first 3 seconds, which is directly useful for your short-form video output (https://www.reddit.com/r/InstagramEmpire/comments/1xypvku/if_youre_new_on_instagram_focus_on_reels_first).
